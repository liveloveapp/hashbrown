# Privacy

Hashbrown is built by LiveLoveApp, LLC.

## Get unstuck

When you fill in the [Get unstuck form](/unstuck), we keep your email address and your question. We use this information to answer your question.

We send an acknowledgement followed by three emails from Brian. Replies go to a human, and every email has an unsubscribe link. Replying "unsubscribe" or "stop" also ends the emails; we read a reply only to check for that and do not store it.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning your information, email [brian@hashbrown.dev](mailto:brian@hashbrown.dev).

## Email

To stop receiving email from us, use the unsubscribe link in an email or contact [brian@hashbrown.dev](mailto:brian@hashbrown.dev).
